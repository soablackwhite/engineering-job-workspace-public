# Mailbox account and privacy boundary

See EMAIL_RECONCILIATION.md for the current generic protocol. Mailbox account labels, aliases, sender exclusions, query plans, message bodies and audit checkpoints are operator data. Configure them privately; no personal or work mailbox is hardcoded in this repository.

Verify the connected identity before reading messages. On missing access, account mismatch or rate limits, preserve progress and follow the operator's current policy without bypassing access restrictions. Do not interpret missing email as an absent prior application.

Keep read/coverage evidence separate from reviewed event identity. Apply only reviewed proposals under current service revisions and read them back. Publication of source does not enable reconciliation or any recurring schedule.
